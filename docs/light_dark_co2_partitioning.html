Biochar greenhouse · CO₂ flux

Light–dark CO₂ partitioning

Each pot is closed in the chamber twice in a row: once in the light, once in the dark. The light window measures the net result of photosynthesis and respiration; the dark window measures respiration alone. Subtracting one from the other gives the CO₂ the plants actually fixed.

What happens inside the chamber

From two measurements to three fluxes

NEE · light window−3.11

Net ecosystem exchange: photosynthesis minus all respiration. Negative means the pot is taking CO₂ out of the air.

ER · dark window+0.78

Ecosystem respiration: shoots, roots and soil microbes together. Always positive; CO₂ builds up in the chamber.

GPP · calculated3.89

Gross primary production: CO₂ fixed by photosynthesis. Not measured directly; it is the gap between the other two.

GPP = ER − NEE = 0.78 − (−3.11) = 3.89 µmol CO₂ m⁻² s⁻¹

Things this method assumes

  • The dark window really is dark. The protocol does not say whether the chamber was covered or the lights switched off; light leaking in would make ER look smaller.
  • Respiration in the light equals respiration in the dark. Leaves respire somewhat less in light, so GPP from this subtraction is an approximation.
  • The two windows see the same conditions. They run back to back, about 10 minutes each, so temperature and moisture are close but not identical.
  • Daytime snapshots. Daily totals need day length: the protocol gives 14–16 h light and 8–10 h dark.
